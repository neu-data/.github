## About Neudata

Neudata Consulting Ltd is a data science, biostatistics and analytics consultancy supporting organisations, researchers and decision-makers in converting complex information into practical insight.

We combine rigorous statistical methods, reproducible analytical workflows and clear communication to support research, programme implementation and evidence-informed decision-making.

Our work spans the complete data lifecycle—from study design and data-management planning to statistical analysis, visualisation, reporting and capacity development.

## What we do

### Data science and advanced analytics

- Exploratory and predictive data analysis
- Machine-learning applications
- Statistical modelling and simulation
- Interactive dashboards and decision-support tools
- Automated and reproducible analytical workflows

### Biostatistics and research support

- Study design and statistical analysis planning
- Sample-size and power calculations
- Clinical, epidemiological and public-health analysis
- Survival and longitudinal data analysis
- Statistical programming and validation
- Manuscript-ready tables, figures and reports

### Bioinformatics and multi-omics

### Data systems and quality assurance

- Automated reporting systems
- Research-data governance and documentation

### Training and capacity development

- R and Python programming
- Biostatistics and statistical inference
- Clinical and public-health data analysis
- Reproducible research
- Data visualisation and scientific reporting

## Our approach

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Scientific rigour** | Methods are selected and documented according to the research question and data structure. |
| **Reproducibility** | Analyses are organised so that results can be reviewed, repeated and updated. |
| **Clarity** | Complex findings are communicated through accessible explanations and purposeful visualisations. |
| **Collaboration** | Solutions are developed with clients and adapted to their operational context. |
| **Capacity strengthening** | Projects are designed to leave teams with useful skills, documentation and sustainable workflows. |
